## Example (Meeting Cost Timer)

**App name:** Meeting Meter

**What it does:** Shows in real time what an ongoing meeting costs.

**Who it's for:** Everyone who sits in too many meetings.

**MVP:** Enter number of participants and average salary → start timer → watch the cost tick upward → stop.

**Explicitly NOT in scope today:** History, export, login, multiple simultaneous meetings.

**Nice-to-have:** Fun milestone messages ("this meeting has now cost a company bike").
